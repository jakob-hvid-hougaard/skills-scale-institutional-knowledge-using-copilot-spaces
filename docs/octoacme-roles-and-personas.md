# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Engagement Lead

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Engagement Lead is responsible for identifying, managing, and communicating with external and internal stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle. They own communication strategies, ensure stakeholder concerns are addressed, and maintain alignment between organizational objectives and project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and map all project stakeholders and their interests
- Develop and execute comprehensive communication plans tailored to different stakeholder groups
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and feedback sessions
- Address stakeholder concerns and escalate issues as needed
- Ensure regular updates and transparency across all stakeholder groups
- Facilitate stakeholder alignment on project decisions and trade-offs
- Manage expectations and build stakeholder confidence

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Collaborates on communication schedules and escalation protocols; provides stakeholder feedback to inform risk management and planning
- **Product Manager**: Ensures stakeholder voice is incorporated into product decisions and roadmap priorities
- **Developers**: Bridges communication between technical team and non-technical stakeholders to explain capabilities and constraints

### Goals
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Prevent communication gaps that lead to scope creep or misaligned expectations
- Build trust and alignment across all project participants
- Enable informed decision-making through transparent stakeholder communication

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and updates (weekly/bi-weekly)
- Communication plans and stakeholder matrices
- Feedback summaries and concern logs
- Executive and board-level status reports

---

## Technical Risk Manager

### Role Summary
The Technical Risk Manager specializes in identifying, assessing, and mitigating technical risks throughout the project lifecycle. They work closely with the Project Manager, technical leads, and Developers to prevent technology-related blockers and ensure solution robustness.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess technical risks early in the project
- Conduct technical feasibility reviews and architectural assessments
- Develop mitigation strategies for identified technical risks
- Monitor technical dependencies and integration points
- Escalate critical technical risks to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Maintain a technical risk register with status tracking
- Provide guidance on technology choices and trade-offs
- Coordinate proof-of-concepts or spike work to reduce uncertainty

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Escalates critical risks and informs overall project risk management strategy
- **Developers**: Collaborates on risk mitigation approaches, technical design reviews, and solution validation
- **Product Manager**: Advises on feasibility of features and helps prioritize work based on technical risk

### Goals
- Prevent critical technical failures and project delays
- Enable proactive decision-making through early risk identification
- Ensure technical solutions meet performance, scalability, and maintainability standards
- Reduce rework and unplanned effort through sound technical planning

### Typical Communication
- Technical risk assessments and feasibility reports
- Risk register updates and escalation reports
- Design review participation and recommendations
- Technical spike planning and retrospectives

---

## Resource Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Resource Coordinator manages resource allocation, capacity planning, and scheduling to ensure project teams have the people and tools they need when needed. They bridge project management and operational teams to support effective execution.

### Responsibilities
- Assess and forecast resource needs based on project plans
- Coordinate resource requests with operational and team leads
- Manage resource conflicts and capacity constraints
- Maintain resource allocation schedules and timelines
- Track resource utilization and identify bottlenecks
- Facilitate cross-project resource planning and prioritization
- Support team onboarding and ramp-up activities

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Provides resource availability and capacity data to inform project planning and risk management
- **Developers**: Ensures team has the skills, tools, and environment needed to complete work efficiently
- **Product Manager**: Coordinates resource needs for different product initiatives and features

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across projects
- Minimize delays caused by resource constraints or unavailability
- Support team productivity and reduce context-switching
- Enable predictable project delivery through effective capacity planning

### Typical Communication
- Resource forecasts and allocation plans
- Capacity reports and bottleneck identification
- Cross-project resource coordination meetings
- Team readiness and onboarding checklists

---

## Quality Assurance Owner

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Owner oversees quality standards, acceptance criteria validation, and quality gates throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure all deliverables meet defined standards before release and champion a culture of quality throughout the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for deliverables
- Establish quality gates and validation processes
- Oversee testing strategy and test coverage
- Validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Provide guidance on quality trade-offs and risk acceptance
- Lead quality retrospectives and continuous improvement efforts
- Ensure compliance with regulatory or organizational quality requirements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on testing approaches, quality reviews, and defect resolution
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on quality standards and acceptance criteria for features
- **Project Manager**: Tracks quality metrics and advises on quality-related risks and delays

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, defect-free solutions
- Prevent quality issues from reaching production
- Build customer trust through consistent quality standards
- Enable continuous improvement through quality metrics and retrospectives

### Typical Communication
- Quality standards and acceptance criteria documentation
- Test plans and quality metrics reports
- Defect tracking and resolution logs
- Quality reviews and gate approvals
- Quality retrospectives and improvement recommendations

---

## Change Management Officer

### Role Summary
The Change Management Officer coordinates organizational change initiatives tied to project deliverables. They manage change impact assessments, stakeholder adoption strategies, and organizational readiness to ensure successful transition to new solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Assess organizational change impact of project deliverables
- Develop change management and adoption strategies
- Create communication and training plans for end-users
- Identify and address resistance to change
- Coordinate change rollout and cutover activities
- Track change adoption metrics and address challenges
- Support organizational learning and capability building
- Ensure alignment between project delivery and organizational readiness

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Stakeholder Engagement Lead**: Collaborates on stakeholder communication and change readiness assessment
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates change activities as part of project delivery plan
- **Product Manager**: Ensures product features are designed for user adoption and organizational readiness

### Goals
- Enable successful organizational adoption of project deliverables
- Minimize disruption and business impact during transitions
- Build user competency and confidence with new solutions
- Measure and sustain benefits realization post-implementation

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and readiness reports
- Communication and training plans
- Adoption metrics and change resistance tracking
- Stakeholder readiness updates and escalations
- Post-implementation review and benefits realization reports

---

## Business Analyst (Extended Role)

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges business and technical domains by capturing requirements, validating business value, and ensuring ongoing alignment between business objectives and project execution. This extended role emphasizes the distinction between initial requirements gathering and continuous business value realization.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct business discovery and requirements gathering
- Document functional and non-functional requirements
- Validate requirements against business objectives and use cases
- Monitor ongoing business alignment throughout project execution
- Identify and assess scope changes and trade-offs
- Ensure requirements remain relevant and value-focused
- Support acceptance testing and user validation
- Provide business context and rationale for decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborates on prioritization and business value assessment
- **Developers**: Clarifies requirements and provides business context during implementation
- **Project Manager**: Advises on business impact of schedule and scope decisions
- **Quality Assurance Owner**: Supports definition of acceptance criteria and validation approach

### Goals
- Ensure project deliverables address real business needs
- Minimize scope creep by maintaining clear requirements and business context
- Enable continuous value realization through ongoing business alignment
- Reduce rework and misalignment through clear, validated requirements

### Typical Communication
- Requirements specifications and business case documentation
- Scope change assessments and impact analysis
- Business alignment reviews and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and validation plans
- Post-implementation business value reviews
